Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TikTok Follower Goal application handles information when you use the application.

Information We Access

The application may access information provided through TikTok's authorized APIs, including basic account information and follower statistics, when you authorize the application.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through TikTok is used only to provide the follower-goal functionality of the application, including displaying follower statistics in a streaming overlay.

Data Storage

We do not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Information is used only as necessary to provide the application's functionality.

Third-Party Services

This application uses TikTok's developer platform and APIs. Your use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

You may stop the application's access to your TikTok account by revoking the application's authorization through your TikTok account settings.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the application owner through the contact information associated with this application.
